## About Me

I'm a PhD student in machine learning at the Technical University of Munich, advised by [Stephan Günnemann](https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/en/daml/team/damlguennemann/) in the [Data Analytics and Machine Learning group](https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/en/daml/home/). My PhD is supported by [relAI](https://zuseschoolrelai.de/).

My research focuses on **generative modeling and foundation models for structured data**, particularly tabular data, relational databases, and graphs. I'm currently working toward diffusion-based tabular foundation models with in-context generation and native support for both generative and predictive tasks.

I collaborate with [SAP Business AI Research](https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/research.html) on my PhD research. Previously, I worked at [QuantCo](https://www.quantco.com/), [Helsing](https://helsing.ai/), and [TNG](https://www.tngtech.com/).

**I'm looking for research internships in 2027** and am open to opportunities across machine learning. Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:a.ketata@tum.de).
